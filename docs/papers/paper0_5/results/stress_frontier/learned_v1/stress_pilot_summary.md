# Paper 0.5 stress pilot

The parameter-matched pilot completed 5568 evaluations. Only 2 of 6 architecture/seed baseline cells crossed the 0.8 competence gate. No dataset cell was competent in all three seeds. Consequently predictive-order, raw-length, nuisance, and span frontiers are not yet estimable: the supported result is a training/optimization failure at the nominal budget. A matched T2 budget rescue is required before interpreting stress-factor slopes.
