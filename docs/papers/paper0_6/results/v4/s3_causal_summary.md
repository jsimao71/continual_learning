# S3 causal completion

For order-3 recipients, matched order-2 update replacement causes mean margin damage 3.6449, compared with 4.3543 for an unrelated same-target donor and 6.2256 for a cross-target donor. The ordered contrast supports partial reuse of order-2 computation inside order-3, but the damage from even matched donors shows that reuse is role- and context-sensitive rather than freely substitutable. This inference is bounded to the competent S3 generator.
